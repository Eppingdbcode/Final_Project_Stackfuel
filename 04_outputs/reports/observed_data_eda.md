# Observed-data MVP findings

All statements are descriptive and preserve source scope.

- WIdO 2024: the four A10BJ ingredients total 2,674.0 thousand prescriptions, 277,936.5 thousand DDD and EUR 582,169.1 thousand net costs. Semaglutide has the largest prescription count and net cost in this acquired cross-section. This is not a trend or indication analysis.
- RKI/GEDA: published age-standardized adult obesity prevalence rises from 12.2% (95% CI 11.5-12.9) in 2003/2004 to 19.7% (18.6-21.0) in 2023. Self-report and survey/mode differences remain material limitations.
- Destatis population: the acquired table covers year-end resident population by age for 2021-2025; the published 2025 total is 83,467,117 persons. It is not a GKV denominator.
- Destatis costs: diabetes E10-E14 all-payer costs are EUR 7,681 million in 2020 and EUR 9,685 million in 2023. E65-E68 costs are EUR 977 million and EUR 1,230 million. These values are contextual, not GKV savings or avoidable costs.
- RKI Diabetes: not acquired and absent from the analytical package.

No causal relationship, eligible population, treated population, avoided cases, net budget impact, ROI or break-even result is calculated.
