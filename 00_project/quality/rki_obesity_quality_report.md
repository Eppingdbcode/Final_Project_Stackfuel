# RKI/GEDA obesity quality report

- Two official PDFs are checksum-validated.
- The analytical table transcribes the `Adipositas — Gesamt` row estimates and 95% confidence intervals from Table 2 on PDF page 6 of DOI `10.25646/12990`.
- Coverage: nine published survey periods from 2003/2004 through 2023; adults aged 18+; Germany.
- Values are weighted, directly age-standardized official survey estimates.
- Missing values: none in the selected total series. Key `date_key` is unique.
- Limitation: height and weight are self-reported and may underestimate obesity; survey prevalence is not clinical eligibility; periods and survey modes vary.
